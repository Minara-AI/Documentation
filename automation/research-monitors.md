# Research monitors


{% hint style="info" %}
Automation is available only in the Minara desktop app. The web app and mobile app do not support it yet.
{% endhint %}

Research monitors run Minara's analysis on a schedule and send you only what matters: upcoming catalysts, shifting momentum, fresh ideas, or new evidence about something you own.

## Templates

These templates are available under **Start from a template** in **Automations**. Clicking one drops a ready-made prompt into the chat so you can adjust it before Minara builds the automation.

| Template                         | What it does                                                              | Starting prompt                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Catalyst calendar**            | Tracks upcoming earnings, dividends, and major economic events            | *"Every week, check my watchlist for upcoming earnings, dividends, and major economic events, and send me a short heads-up."* |
| **Sector rotation monitor**      | Shows which sectors and themes are gaining or losing momentum             | *"Every week, tell me which market sectors and themes money is moving into and out of, and send me a summary."*               |
| **Idea screener**                | Sends a weekly shortlist of stocks and tokens that match your filters     | *"Every week, find stocks and tokens that pass my filters, leave out what I already own, and send me a fresh shortlist."*     |
| **Thesis tracker**               | Alerts you when new evidence challenges your reason for holding something | *"Watch the story behind my holdings and let me know when something changes that could break the original reason I bought."*  |
| **Prediction-market odds alert** | Alerts you when Polymarket odds cross your threshold                      | *"Alert me when a Polymarket market's odds cross a threshold I set, and explain what it could mean for the market."*          |

## More examples

* *"Check NVDA's SEC filings page every 30 minutes and alert me on Discord when a new filing appears."*
* *"Every Monday, review Solana's 10 most active projects by wallet growth and send me the top changes."*
* *"Every two weeks, compare L1, L2, and DeFi tokens and suggest whether I should rebalance."*

Minara can open web pages in a browser as part of an automation, so it can watch sites that don't offer alerts of their own.

## Set it up

1. In the desktop app, open **Automations** and pick a template, or describe the monitor in the **Chat** tab.
2. Tell Minara what to watch (a watchlist, your holdings, a sector, a URL), how often to check, what counts as worth telling you, and where to send it.
3. Click **Test** to see a sample report, then refine it in the chat, for example *"keep it under 5 bullet points"*.

## Tips

* **Define "worth telling me".** A clear rule, such as "only if guidance changes" or "only new filings", keeps messages short and useful.
* **Mind the frequency.** Each check that uses AI analysis consumes credits. Weekly or daily checks suit most research.

## Limitations

* AI analysis can be incomplete or wrong. Treat these reports as research input, not investment advice.
* Website monitors depend on the page staying reachable and keeping a similar layout.
* The desktop app must be running for scheduled checks to happen.
